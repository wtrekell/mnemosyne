# Obsidian

Markdown editor the [[llm-wiki]] author keeps open beside the LLM agent to browse results live: follow links, check graph view, read updated pages. Ecosystem pieces mentioned: Web Clipper (articles to markdown), attachment-folder setting plus a download hotkey for local images (e.g. `raw/assets/`), graph view (hubs and orphans), Dataview (queries over frontmatter), and a Marp plugin (slide decks). Role in the [[llm-wiki-pattern]]: the IDE.
